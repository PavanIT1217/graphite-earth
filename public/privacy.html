Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Graphite Earth draws 3D city sketches on your device. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Data sent to third parties

  • Place search: the text you type is sent to Nominatim (OpenStreetMap Foundation). See the OSMF privacy policy.
  • Map data: the bounding box of the area you view is sent to a public Overpass API server.

Data stored on your device

Downloaded map data and search results are cached in your browser's/app's local storage (IndexedDB) for up to 14 days so repeat visits are fast and the free services aren't overloaded. Exported images are saved only where you choose.
