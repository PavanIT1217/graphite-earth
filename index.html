    Graphite Earth

    The whole Earth, drawn as a pencil, charcoal or blueprint sketch. Spin the globe and zoom in anywhere — the map sharpens as you go, and below 3 km the streets under you rise up as a 3D drawing of the real buildings. In a city, drag to orbit, use two fingers (or right-drag) to pan, and zoom out to return to the globe.

    Data & attribution

    Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence. Globe map tiles from the OpenStreetMap tile servers; place search by Nominatim; buildings, roads, water and parks via the Overpass API. Requests are rate-limited and cached on your device to respect these free services.

    How it's drawn

    Footprints are extruded into merged meshes. A post-processing shader finds edges in the depth and normal buffers, hatches surfaces by how much sun they get (cross-hatching in shadow) and lays it all on textured paper.
