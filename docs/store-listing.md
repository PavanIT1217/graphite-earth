## Full description

The whole world, drawn by hand.

Graphite Earth turns real OpenStreetMap data into a living sketchbook. Spin a pencil-drawn globe, zoom
in anywhere, and watch coastlines, roads and neighbourhoods sharpen into ink. Keep going and the streets
rise up as a 3D drawing of the actual buildings — every outline inked, every shadow cross-hatched.

• A full 3D globe — zoom from space down to street level, anywhere on Earth
• Real buildings, heights, roads, rivers, parks and beaches from OpenStreetMap
• Three drawing styles: pencil, charcoal and blueprint
• Move the sun and watch the hatching follow the shadows
• "Sketchy" mode makes the lines gently boil like an animated drawing
• Export high-resolution PNG art of any view
• Search any place, or fly to Manhattan, Paris, Shibuya or Visakhapatnam
• No account, no ads, no tracking

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence.
